My story

I'm 27 and live in Harlingen, the Netherlands. At FOV, as the only data specialist, I built a marketing intelligence platform. From raw API data to dashboards, forecasting models and pipelines that run on their own.

Before that I ran my own sportswear brand, led a team in a supermarket and helped business owners at Rabobank. You see that mix in my work: I think like an entrepreneur and build like an engineer.
